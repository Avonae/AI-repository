## Personal notes

- Personal notes and instructions written for me follow the language I write in, like chat replies. In such notes:
  - Keep them short.
  - In Russian, use «должен» / «не должен» / «следует» instead of MUST / MUST NOT / SHOULD.
  - Title headings with a noun phrase («Установка плагина»), not an imperative («Установи плагин»).
  - An instruction note contains only the steps. Do not describe how the result behaves afterwards.
  - Drop any sentence that neither tells me what to do nor gives a fact I need (e.g. «Без них в шаге 4 нечего будет включать»).
- Save personal notes to `claude-knowledge/notes/` in the Obsidian vault (server: `/opt/docker/apps/syncthing/config/obsidian_vault`, Linux desktop: `/home/alex/Sync/Obsidian-vault`) unless I name
  another folder. `Блог/` in that vault is for blog drafts only.
